PREPARATION, NOT PROMISES

You can’t make every experience predictable.
You can help make it more familiar.

Instead of explaining everything for the first time in a busy waiting room, unfamiliar classroom, airport, or barber chair, start preparing beforehand. Review the Ready Pack at home. Bring it with you. Share the support guide with the person helping them.
