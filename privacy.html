Privacy Policy

AutismReady does not currently create user accounts or intentionally save the information entered into the Ready Pack builder on an AutismReady server. Information entered into the current builder is used in your browser to create the preview and purchased Ready Pack. AutismReady does not currently provide saved profiles or accounts, so you should download or print your finished pack before leaving the purchase flow.

Payment checkout is processed by Stripe. AutismReady sends checkout information needed to complete the purchase to Stripe; payment-card details are entered with Stripe rather than into the Ready Pack builder. If photo uploads, saved profiles, accounts, analytics, or other storage features are added, this policy will be updated before those features are offered.

Do not enter medical records, insurance information, diagnoses, or other sensitive health information. The builder is designed to work with practical preferences such as communication methods, sensory considerations, and supports.
