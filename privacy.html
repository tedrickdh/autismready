Privacy Policy

AutismReady does not currently create user accounts or intentionally save the information entered into the Ready Pack builder on an AutismReady server. Information entered into the current builder is used in the browser to create the preview and is cleared when the session ends or the page is refreshed.

Payment checkout is not yet active. Before payment processing, accounts, analytics, uploads, or saved profiles are enabled, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant providers, information collected, purposes, retention, choices, and deletion options.

Do not enter medical records, insurance information, diagnoses, or other sensitive health information. The builder is designed to work with practical preferences such as communication methods, sensory considerations, and supports.
